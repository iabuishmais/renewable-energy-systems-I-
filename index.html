Ideal inverting buck–boost converter operating in continuous conduction, feeding a fixed 24 V battery (constant-voltage approximation). The output is the voltage magnitude; its actual polarity is negative. Losses, switching transients and battery SoC dynamics are excluded.

Educational quasi-static model, not a detailed switching or electrochemical simulation. Uniform mode uses an approximate PV I–V characteristic. Partial-shading mode represents two series PV sections with ideal bypass action on the weaker section; it intentionally produces two power maxima.
